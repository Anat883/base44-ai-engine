# Creative Brief — MasterChef Pink Cup

## Goal

A 30-second product promotional video for the MasterChef Pink Cup — a premium kitchen accessory. The video should feel warm, aspirational, and cinematic.

## Story

A chef reaches for the pink cup, pours a perfect espresso, steps outside into golden morning light, and savours the moment. The cup is the hero.

## Mood

- Warm, golden hour lighting
- Slow, deliberate camera movements
- Quiet and intimate — no voiceover, only ambient sound and soft music
- Colour palette: blush pink, warm gold, cream

## Shots (6)

1. Close-up: hand reaches for the pink cup on a marble counter
2. Medium: espresso pours into the cup — steam rises
3. Close-up: cup lifted, liquid swirls
4. Wide: chef walks through a sunlit kitchen doorway
5. Outdoor medium: chef sits, cup in both hands, light catches the pink glaze
6. Hero close-up: cup centred in frame, logo visible, slow pull back

## Deliverable

30-second video, 16:9, 1080p. Suitable for Instagram Reels and paid social.
