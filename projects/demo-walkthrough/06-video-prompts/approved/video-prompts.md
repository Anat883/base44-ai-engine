# Video Prompts — MasterChef Pink Cup

## Shot 1 — Hand reaches for cup

Animate from first to last frame. Slow, deliberate hand movement. Soft focus background. Warm kitchen light. No camera movement.

## Shot 2 — Espresso pours

Animate the pour: dark liquid falling in a thin stream, steam rising in slow curls. Slight camera drift downward to follow the stream. Golden backlight.

## Shot 3 — Cup lifted

Slow upward movement of the cup. Liquid moves gently. Camera follows the lift with a subtle tilt up.

## Shot 4 — Chef walks through doorway

Smooth walk, unhurried. Light transitions from cool interior to warm golden exterior as the chef crosses the threshold. Camera static.

## Shot 5 — Chef sits outdoors

Gentle settle into seat. Cup catches the light as the chef wraps both hands around it. Very slight camera push in. Warm bokeh background.

## Shot 6 — Hero pull back

Start tight on the pink glaze and logo. Slowly pull back to reveal the outdoor setting. End with cup small in a large golden frame. Camera move is the only motion.
